# Evaluating Whether to Escalate

Three tests. Apply them in order.

**1. Bottleneck test.** Is the gap in reasoning or in information? If gathering more data would resolve the uncertainty, do that first. If uncertainty persists after you have the facts, it's a reasoning gap — escalate.

**2. Cost asymmetry.** Escalate when the cost of being wrong outweighs the cost of asking. Don't escalate when the decision is easily reversible or the stakes are low.

**3. Timing.** Escalate at the moment you feel the uncertainty, not after committing to a direction. The advisor sees less context early and can redirect cheaply. After you've built something, the advice costs more and changes less.
